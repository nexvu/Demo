A salon for the days that matter, and the ordinary ones too

Meera's – The Beauty Saga is a beauty salon and academy in Sudama Nagar, Indore, founded and run by Nikita and Pinky.

Some visits are for a wedding morning. Others are a quick tidy-up before the week begins. Either way, the idea is the same: listen first, take the time the work needs, and send you home looking like yourself on a very good day.

Makeup, skin, hair and nails are all done here, and for the days you can't come in, the team can come to you.
